Summer Internship Blog

Week One (May 27 – 29)

My first week interning with Kate & Co. has been a very informative and exciting experience that has introduced me to the company’s day-to-day operations and how the team effectively collaborates to meet client needs. I got set up with many of the company’s platforms and tools they use to communicate and manage projects, which helped me to further my understanding of workflow processes and how tasks are assigned. I also had the opportunity to sit in on the weekly virtual all-team call, where I learned about ongoing projects and what each team member was currently working on. One of my favorite opportunities this week was contributing to some graphic design work for clients using Canva. Although my experience with Canva was quite limited, I was able to look through past designs and see how the team maintains brand consistency through the use of color schemes, typography, and layout. This hands-on experience allowed me to develop new graphic design skills and gave me the confidence to handle similar tasks in the future. Overall I’m very pleased with how my first week went and am excited for what’s to come!


Week Two (June 3 – 5)

This week, I was presented with many new work opportunities and tasks that have helped me become more familiar with company operations. I worked on sourcing and filling out information for a New Jersey Legislature spreadsheet for a new client. I also got to sit in on a meeting discussing the redesign of a client website (a project I will more than likely be contributing to), where we discussed the site’s current content and design flaws and how they could be improved. Lastly, I began editing some video content that will eventually be posted on client social media over the next couple of weeks. Next week, I will be joining the team on a trip to Harrisburg to gather some content at a rally event, which I’m very much looking forward to!


Week Three (June 9 - 12)

This week I tagged along with the content creation team gathering social media content for the Fight for the Future rally for working-class people in Harrisburg. We visited the PA AFL-CIO Headquarters, where rally members met before marching to the State Capitol Building steps to demand a future where all working families come first. In addition to gathering picture and video coverage, we also conducted interviews with union/rally members to feature on client social media. This trip allowed me to experience firsthand how the team effectively works together to capture content in a fast-paced environment. It was also a great opportunity to witness the rally up close, in person, and its powerful message. 


Week Four (June 17 - 18)

This week, I continued working on editing video content by transcribing dialogue and adding subtitles to clips that will be posted on client social media. I also spent some time adding new information to the New Jersey Legislature spreadsheet and scheduling additional social media posts. My report is shorter this week, as I had one day off. 


Week Five (June 24 - 26)

This week was quite similar in terms of work to last week. I finished up editing the video content that I was assigned last week and continued updating the New Jersey Legislature spreadsheet. I also started working on editing a short Instagram reel of clips from the AFL-CIO union rally set to empowering music, aiming to capture its impactfulness. I was also invited one afternoon to run some errands with a co-worker, which included a trip to the post office, bank, and office supply store.


Week Six (July 1 - 3)

This week, I finished making the Instagram reel of clips from the AFL-CIO union rally. Afterward, I was assigned to make more Instagram Reels from the interview footage we gathered at the event. For each interview reel, I transcribed and added subtitles along with background music. I thoroughly enjoyed the editing process of these reels because I was given a lot of creative freedom when it came to the text effects and animation. I incorporated the brand’s colors through text backgrounds and added subtle animations to enhance visuals while maintaining a professional look.


Week Seven (July 8 - 10)

At the beginning of this week, I tagged along with a co-worker to help set up and prepare for the VNET Tech Summit 2025 held at the Bayfront Convention Center. This included gathering and setting up company-designed print materials like flyers, posters, and signs that were utilized in various spaces throughout the convention center. We also helped by placing event favors around the tables in the ballroom and organizing the merchandise table display. The next day, we attended the event and gathered photo and video content of keynote speakers and attendee interactions. Not only was this event a great opportunity to practice capturing on-site content, but it was also a learning experience for all that goes into major event planning and brand presentation.


Week Eight (July 15 - 17)

This week, I added some final touches and finished up the AFL-CIO union rally interview reels. I also started working on a fast-cut reel with footage we captured from the VNET Tech Summit. I had some downtime this week, where I continued to update the New Jersey Legislature spreadsheet.


Week Nine (July 22 - 24)

This week, I was assigned the task of transferring important project information from the website currently being redesigned into a text document. This had to be done manually, as the site had the project information displayed in a way that couldn’t easily be copied over into a text document without formatting issues. During the middle of the week, I had the opportunity to attend an in-person meeting at the VNET building for a debrief of this year's tech summit. It was a very informative and valuable experience, as I got to listen to discussions and feedback about what went well and what could be improved for next year’s event.


Week Ten (July 29 - 31)

This week, I had the opportunity to attend a press conference hosted by ECGRA at the Film Erie headquarters to announce investments within Erie’s Arts, Culture, and Heritage organizations. I was tasked with creating a slideshow of the grant recipients to play at the beginning of the event. Aside from following brand guidelines, I was given creative freedom on the project, which made it even more rewarding to see the slideshow displayed in person and witness the attendees' positive reactions.


Week Eleven (August 5 - 7)

This week, I was tasked with contacting venues to explore options for hosting an annual reception for a client company. The goal was to ensure that each space met specific requirements and was available on the intended date. I also worked on updating a spreadsheet that tracks the yearly media mentions of another client company. The spreadsheet includes different pages for each year and records details such as the date, link, news outlet, headline, sentiment rating, coverage type (article or video), and topic. The main goal of the spreadsheet is to be able to compare the media mentions for the years before and after the company became a client. It was insightful to see the transformation in the company's media presence and how the media mentions became more frequent over time.


Week Twelve (August 12 - 14)

Off for vacation.
